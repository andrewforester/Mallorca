# Her upper body (shoulders and chest)

## Goal

Her MetaHuman face stops at the neck. She gets a seated upper body in a mustard crew-neck sweater, framed to mid-chest,
moved by the existing `Pose.spine` and `Pose.shoulder`. The arms, hands and the full body skeleton ship now, so that arm
poses, gestures and runtime body correctives can come later without changing the asset format.

## Decisions

- **The body is the MetaHuman's own.** `body.dna` of the same Creator DCC export: same character, same proportions, the
  neck seam matches the head, same Epic terms as the head in `web/public/avatar/LICENSE.md`.
- **The sweater is generated by the export script.** A shell offset from the body's torso and arms, a round neckline,
  ribbed edges, a tiling CC0 knit texture and a mustard colour parameter. No Blender, no hand modelling.
- **The seated pose is baked offline.** RigLogic in Python evaluates the body correctives for one seated pose; the
  result is a table of local joint transforms. The geometry stays in the DNA bind pose and skinning bends it.
- **The asset limit rises from 15 MB to 17 MB**, in its own commit.
- Out of scope: runtime body RigLogic, arm and hand animation, IK, other outfits, legs.

## Export: `web/tools/avatar/metahuman/export/`

`body.py` runs from `build.py` after the head and the groom.

### Skeleton

The head DNA starts at `spine_04` and already holds the clavicles, `upperarm_*` and their correctives. The body adds the
joints the head lacks: `root`, `pelvis`, `spine_01` to `spine_03`, the forearms, hands and fingers with their helpers.
Leg joints are left out. Joints with the same name are not duplicated: body vertices refer to the head's joint.
`rig.json` gains:

```json
"body": { "joints": ["root", "pelvis", ...], "parents": ["", "root", ...], "rest": [[tx, ty, tz, rx, ry, rz], ...] }
```

Parents are names, resolved against the head joints and then the body joints. `rest` is the local transform in the DNA
units (cm, degrees, Euler XYZ as RigLogic writes them). The export fails when a joint shared by both DNA files differs
in world position by more than 0.05 cm.

### Body mesh

LOD1 of `body.dna`, without the legs: triangles whose vertices all lie below the pelvis are dropped, the arms and hands
stay. Skin entirely covered by the sweater is dropped too, keeping a 2 cm band under every sweater edge. What remains
visible is the neck, the collarbones inside the neckline and the hands. Skin indices are remapped to the merged joint
list. Normals of vertices that coincide with head vertices on the neck seam are averaged with the head's.

### Sweater mesh

From the body's torso and arm triangles before the cut:

- offset along the vertex normals: 1.2 cm on the torso, 0.8 cm on the sleeves, easing to 0.4 cm at the neckline;
- a round neckline: triangles above a curve through points just above the clavicles are dropped;
- ribbing: the last 1.5 cm at the neckline, the cuffs and the hem gets a separate UV band for the rib texture;
- sleeves end at the wrist, the hem at the hips;
- skin indices and weights are copied from the nearest body vertex;
- UVs: the body's UVs scaled for the knit tile.

### Seated pose

RigLogic (OpenRigLogic Python bindings) reads `body.dna`, the raw controls are set from the seated pose (arms down along
the body and slightly forward, shoulders slightly in), and the joint outputs become `pose.sitting` in `rig.json`: joint
name to local transform, for the arm joints and every corrective joint the solvers move.

### Hair

Hair card vertices that land inside the sweater or the body in the seated pose are pushed out along the surface normal
to 3 mm outside it.

### Textures

- `body_color_map.webp` 1024 px: `Maps/Body_Basecolor.png` colour-matched to `Synth/BASECOLOR` of the head in Lab, the
  statistics taken on the neck region both maps cover. `body_normal_map.webp` 1024 px from `Maps/Body_Normal.png`.
- `knit_normal_map.webp`, `knit_roughness_map.webp` 512 px tiling, from a CC0 knit material (ambientCG or Poly Haven),
  credited in `LICENSE.md`. The colour is a constant in the material.

### Checks

`--check` also decodes the body sections and fails over 17 MB.

## Browser: `web/src/ui/avatar/rig/`

- `assets.ts`: optional `body` and `pose` in `RigHeader`. A header without them loads as today.
- `RigState`: body bones are created after the head bones and parented by name; the head's `spine_04` is attached to
  `spine_03`. Each frame: RigLogic for the face, then `pose.sitting` replaces the local transforms of its joints, then
  `Pose.spine` and `Pose.shoulder` on top. Neck joints stay moved by `Pose.neck` only.
- `RigAvatar`: meshes `body` and `sweater` are built like the others. Body gets the head skin material without
  wrinkles; the sweater gets a fabric material in a new `fabric.ts`: tiling knit normal and roughness, sheen.
- `stage.ts`: `frameHead` becomes a bust frame: the same headroom, down to about mid-chest. The numbers come from the
  phone and desktop contact sheets.
- Fallbacks are unchanged: the Rocketbox glb, then the portrait. `portrait.webp` is redrawn with the body.

## Testing

- Python `--check`: shared joints coincide, decode round trip, size.
- Unit: `RigState` with and without a body section (parents by name, the seated table applied, poses on top),
  decoding of the new sections.
- `npm test`, `npm run test:e2e` (the seat reaches `data-face="live"`), contact sheets, and
  `tools/avatar/metahuman/fps.ts` on desktop and on a throttled phone.
- Intermediate renders shown to the owner after each stage: pose and body, sweater, skin tone, the live face in the game.
